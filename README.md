## The entry

`main.ts` (pure Node standard library, no dependencies) writes one monologue
of exactly 100 words to `/output/entry.txt`. The speaker is the Toll-Keeper of
a mountain pass: thirty years stamping the same nine tolls, offering tea to the
hero who came to kill him, and handing over the crown — provided the ledger
gets signed first. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small
deflections (the sighing step, the kettle, shutting his eyes against the
lamplight), never in a plain statement that he is weary. The sting lands in
the last line: he closes his eyes not for mercy, but because the lamplight
hurts them now.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt (100 words, 549 characters)
```
